# Investigation Feasibility Matrix — Topic × Equipment × Time × Level

Structured reference for Mode D (lesson practical design) and Mode A (IA design guidance — what's feasible with what the school has). Each entry names the investigation, the equipment it requires, the time estimate, the level (SL/HL), and a data-quality rating.

**How to use**: when the teacher names a topic or asks "what can we do for [understanding]?", cross-reference with the equipment-inventory skill (if maintained) or the teacher's description. Filter by time available and level. Present options ranked by data quality.

## Biology

| Investigation | Equipment needed | Time | Level | Data quality | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Enzyme catalysis** (effect of temperature/pH on catalase) | Water bath, gas syringe OR mass loss (balance + H₂O₂), stopwatch | 2–3 h | SL | ★★★★★ | Fast, replicable, clear trend. Classic IA topic — needs a unique angle |
| **Osmosis** (potato/sucrose concentration) | Balance (0.01g), beakers, sucrose solutions, potato cores, ruler | 2–3 h | SL | ★★★★ | Reliable; mass change or length change. Pre-cut cores for consistency |
| **Photosynthesis rate** (Elodea/aquatic plant) | Light source, ruler, stopwatch, thermometer, beaker, NaHCO₃ | 2–3 h | SL | ★★★ | Counting bubbles is subjective; gas syringe is better. Light distance as IV |
| **Photosynthesis** (colorimetric — DPIP/DCPIP) | Colorimeter (635 nm), cuvettes, chloroplast extract, DPIP | 3–4 h | HL | ★★★★ | Quantitative; requires chloroplast extraction. Good for HL |
| **Heart rate** (Daphnia/ethanol) | Microscope, stopwatch, ethanol solutions, Daphnia culture | 2–3 h | SL | ★★★ | Living organism — ethics approval needed. Counting heartbeats is skill-dependent |
| **Transpiration rate** (potometer) | Potometer, ruler, fan, lamp, Vaseline | 2–3 h | SL | ★★★ | Tricky to set up; air bubbles can be unreliable. Better with mass loss method |
| **Effect of salt on plant cells** (onion epidermis) | Microscope, salt solutions, slides, coverslips, ruler/eyepiece graticule | 1–2 h | SL | ★★★ | Qualitative/semi-quantitative. Plasmolysis scoring is subjective |
| **Biodiversity** (Simpson's/Shannon-Wiener) | Quadrat, transect, identification keys, calculator | 3–4 h (field) | SL | ★★★★ | Fieldwork; weather-dependent. Good for database IAs too |
| **Effect of light on seed germination** | Seeds (cress/radish), Petri dishes, filter paper, light/dark boxes | 1 week+ | SL | ★★★ | Long time frame; count germinated seeds. Temperature must be controlled |
| **Yeast fermentation** (CO₂ production) | Water bath, gas syringe or inverted tube, yeast, glucose, stopwatch | 2–3 h | SL | ★★★★ | Fast data; temperature or sugar concentration as IV |
| **Effect of exercise on heart rate** | Stopwatch (or heart rate monitor), step/bench | 1–2 h | SL | ★★★ | Human participants — consent forms needed. PAR-Q for vigorous exercise |
| **Microbial growth** (zones of inhibition) | Agar plates, paper discs, antiseptics/disinfectants, ruler | 2–3 h + incubation | SL | ★★★★ | Needs 24–48 h incubation. Aseptic technique required |
| **Chi-squared** (genetic cross / phenotype ratios) | Countable organisms (corn kernels, fruit flies, seeds) | 1–2 h | SL | ★★★★★ | Count data; chi-squared is the natural test. No continuous DV |

## Chemistry

| Investigation | Equipment needed | Time | Level | Data quality | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Acid–base titration** (concentration determination) | Burette, pipette, conical flask, indicator, analytical balance | 2–3 h | SL | ★★★★★ | Concordant titres; the gold standard for chemistry IA uncertainty treatment |
| **Enthalpy change** (calorimetry — displacement/reaction) | Polystyrene cup, thermometer (±0.5°C), balance, reagents | 2–3 h | SL | ★★★ | Heat loss is the main weakness; need lid. Simple but limited precision |
| **Rates: iodine clock** | Stopwatch, solutions (KIO₃, Na₂SO₃, H₂SO₄, starch), thermometer | 2–3 h | SL | ★★★★ | Dramatic endpoint; temperature or concentration as IV. Fast data collection |
| **Rates: gas collection** (H₂ from Mg + HCl) | Gas syringe, conical flask, Mg ribbon, HCl, stopwatch, thermometer | 2–3 h | SL | ★★★★ | Initial rate method (gradient at t=0). Concentration or temperature as IV |
| **Rates: mass loss** (Na₂CO₃ + HCl or marble chips + acid) | Balance (0.01g), conical flask, cotton wool plug, acid, solid | 2–3 h | SL | ★★★★ | CO₂ escapes through cotton wool; mass loss = CO₂ produced |
| **Equilibrium shift** (cobalt chloride / Le Chatelier) | Colorimeter, cuvettes, CoCl₂ solutions, HCl, water | 2–3 h | HL | ★★★★ | Colorimetric monitoring of equilibrium position. Concentration or temperature |
| **Electrochemistry** (cell potentials) | Voltmeter, salt bridge, metal electrodes, solutions | 2–3 h | SL | ★★★ | Compare measured vs theoretical (Nernst). Good for % difference treatment |
| **Spectrophotometry** (Beer-Lambert law) | Colorimeter/spectrophotometer, cuvettes, stock solution | 2–3 h | SL | ★★★★★ | Calibration curve; very precise. Concentration as IV |
| **Effect of temperature on reaction rate** (sodium thiosulfate + HCl) | Solutions, thermometer, conical flask, stopwatch, white paper with cross | 2–3 h | SL | ★★★★ | Disappearing cross method. Classic; needs consistent volumes |
| **Solubility vs temperature** | Balance, beaker, thermometer, hot plate, solute | 3–4 h | SL | ★★★ | Saturation point is subjective. Multiple trials needed |
| **Organic synthesis** (aspirin / ester) | Reagents, reflux/heating, filtration, balance, melting point apparatus | 4–6 h | HL | ★★★ | Yield % and purity (melting point / TLC). Multi-step; time-intensive |

## Physics

| Investigation | Equipment needed | Time | Level | Data quality | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Pendulum** (period vs length) | String, masses, clamp stand, stopwatch, ruler | 2–3 h | SL | ★★★★★ | T² vs L linearisation → g. Classic; reaction time is main uncertainty |
| **Hooke's law** (spring extension) | Springs, masses, ruler/metre stick, clamp stand | 2–3 h | SL | ★★★★★ | F vs x linearisation → k. Simple, precise, clear physics |
| **Resistance** (Ohm's law / resistivity) | Power supply, multimeter, wires, rheostat, ruler, micrometer | 2–3 h | SL | ★★★★ | R vs L or R vs A for resistivity. Contact resistance is a weakness |
| **Specific heat capacity** | Immersion heater, calorimeter, thermometer, power supply, balance, stopwatch | 2–3 h | SL | ★★★ | Heat losses; insulate well. ΔT vs t → power → c |
| **Snell's law** (refraction) | Ray box, protractor, glass/acrylic block, ruler | 2–3 h | SL | ★★★★ | sin i vs sin r → n. Precise angle measurement is key |
| **Capacitor discharge** | Capacitor, resistor, voltmeter (or datalogger), switch, stopwatch | 2–3 h | HL | ★★★★ | ln V vs t → RC. Datalogger greatly improves data quality |
| **Simple harmonic motion** (mass-spring) | Spring, masses, motion detector (or video), ruler | 2–3 h | HL | ★★★★ | T vs √m linearisation. Motion detector gives precise period |
| **Speed of sound** (resonance tube / standing waves) | Tuning forks, tube, water, ruler | 2–3 h | SL | ★★★ | Resonance point is somewhat subjective. Multiple frequencies needed |
| **Projectile motion** | Ramp, ball, metre ruler, stopwatch, carbon paper | 2–3 h | SL | ★★★ | Range vs launch angle or height. Timing is the weak point |
| **Photoelectric effect** | Light source, filters, voltmeter, photocell | 3–4 h | HL | ★★★★ | V vs f → h/e. Needs monochromatic filters. Good for theory comparison |
| **Black body radiation** (inverse square law) | Light sensor, lamp, ruler, colour filters | 2–3 h | SL | ★★★★ | I vs 1/d². Room must be dark; ambient light is a confounder |
| **Magnetic field** (solenoid / Helmholtz coils) | Power supply, compass/magnetometer, ruler, coils | 2–3 h | HL | ★★★ | B vs position or B vs I. Earth's field must be considered |

## Cross-science: Database and simulation investigations

These require no equipment — only a computer and a data source. Suitable for students who cannot access the lab for extended periods.

| Investigation type | Data source | Time | Level | Data quality | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Correlation study** (e.g. HDI vs life expectancy) | Our World in Data, World Bank | 8–10 h | SL | ★★★ | Must use biological/chemical/physical variables. Pearson/Spearman + significance |
| **Climate data analysis** | NOAA, NASA GISS | 8–10 h | SL | ★★★★ | Temperature trends, CO₂ correlation. Large datasets; good for statistics |
| **Species distribution** | IUCN Red List, GBIF | 8–10 h | SL | ★★★ | Ecological variables; chi-squared or correlation. Sampling must be explained |
| **Periodic trends** | NIST, IUPAC data | 8–10 h | SL | ★★★★ | Chemistry: electronegativity, ionisation energy vs atomic number. R² treatment |
| **Simulation** (e.g. population dynamics) | Virtual Biology Lab, NetLogo | 8–10 h | SL | ★★★ | Must explain the model and its limitations. Screenshots in body |

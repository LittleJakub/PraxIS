# What the Examplars Teach — Patterns from the Official Assessed Samples

Distilled in the author's own words from the official IB DP Biology assessed samples (current criteria) that ship with the teacher support material. Every claim below is a pattern observed in the examiner commentaries — the strongest available evidence for what moves marks. Use in Mode A (design scaffolding), Mode B (review comments), and Mode C (marking).

## Research design — what the examiner rewarded

- **A focused RQ with real context.** A repeatedly recurring criticism is that the question needed sharper focus (02, 03, 04); a broad but generic background caps the first strand. 06's focused question, cited standard protocol, and correct nomenclature sat in 5–6.
- **Trialling earns credit.** Range established by trialling (02), preliminary experimentation on timing and stain choice (06) — both praised. An unjustified IV range is a recurring complaint (03, 06).
- **Controls must be real.** 03's 0 g control was not a fair comparison — the same volume of solvent should have been added to the control. Specifics like the type and brand of milk were missing (03); an unmonitored environment and tap water were noted (06).
- **Method reproducibility decides strand 3.** A procedure that is reasonably easy to follow sits near 4; vague or detail-poor descriptions sit at 2–3 (01, 04, 05).
- **Database/simulation work lives or dies on screenshots.** Missing screenshots (05), or screenshots that would have helped (07), or screenshots relegated to the appendix (04) — plus the selection and sampling decision never explained (05). Screenshots go in the body, with the filtering/sampling decision explained.
- **Risk assessment:** missing entirely = noted (01); considered and mitigated = praised (03); for databases/simulations it is not essential (04, 05) — but ethical reflections are to the student's credit.
- **Hypotheses are optional — and must be right if given.** Incomplete hypothesis (03); a hypothesis confused with a prediction (07).
- **Conventions leak marks from day one:** scientific-name format (01), metric units (01, 07), retrieval dates for online sources (01, 04).

## Data analysis — the highest-leverage criterion

- **The significance test is the band-mover.** Where no significance testing was carried out, the processing strand caps at 2–3 (01, 03, 04); 07 was told a proper test would have moved it to the next band; 06's n = 5 was small but earned credit because a test was actually run; 05's testing of correlation coefficients was praised.
- **Test choice must fit the question.** ANOVA was judged inappropriate for a correlation-style question — a correlation coefficient with significance testing would have satisfied it (03). R² describes fit, not correlation (03); regression lines should not be forced to maximize R² (05).
- **Graphs are where marks are lost cheaply.** Wrong axis scales reduce the usefulness of graphs substantially (02, 03); error bars left unidentified (02, 04); units in table cells instead of column headings (01); mixed non-metric units (07). Fixing these is free.
- **Uncertainties: appropriate, not exhaustive.** Percentage uncertainties are not required in biology (02); uncertainties presented but never used in the interpretation earn no credit (02). 06's thorough treatment (ranges, trend lines, R², worked examples) is the model; 01's lone ruler precision is the floor.
- **Decimal places and consistency** — minor inconsistencies cap precision (02); consistent, sensible conventions are what top work earns praise for (06).
- **Sample size awareness:** SD at n = 3 was judged too small (02); small n is survivable when acknowledged and backed by a test (06).
- **Appendices are dead weight for analysis:** examiners are not obliged to read appendices (04); calculations placed in an appendix (03) were ignored. Sample data in the body + rest in the appendix is acceptable (04).
- **Processing ladder:** means + SD + graphs ≈ 3; + trend lines, R², justified significance test, uncertainty interpretation ≈ 5–6. Processing that stays basically descriptive caps strand 3 (04).

## Conclusion — riding on the analysis and the context

- **Weak processing caps the conclusion.** The interpretation is limited by the processing in 01, 03, 04. A conclusion cannot outrun its analysis.
- **Uncertainties must be interpreted, not just calculated.** 02 calculated uncertainties but never used them — leaving them pointless. 06/07 correctly interpreted trend lines, R², and range bars → 5.
- **Scientific context is the second strand, and it separates 4 from 5.** Some relevant scientific context ≈ 3–4 (01, 02); strong context with traceable sources ≈ 5 (06, 07); context that stays generic (wanting biological mechanism — drug resistance, side effects) capped 05 at 4.
- **Caution is rewarded:** no causation claim from correlation (07 praised); more careful wording urged (06, minor). Claim only what the data support.
- **Outliers handled honestly:** 06's removal was a legitimate treatment backed by evidence; never silently drop data.

## Evaluation — specificity and impact, in that order

- **The ladder is generic → specific → relative impact.** Weaknesses identified without any evaluation of their relative impact = 3–4 (01, 04, 05 partially). A fairly limited evaluation = 2 (03). Specific weaknesses identified and discussed with their impact evaluated = 5 (06).
- **Improvements must tie to the identified weaknesses and be precise.** Improvements that lack precision (01, 04), are not always relevant (02, 03), or stay very general (07) all cap the second strand at 3. 06's relevant, sensible, realistic improvements reached 5.
- **Missed weaknesses count against:** 02 ignored that fruit samples hold far more water than chocolate; 06's tap-water fix raised the question of why it was not used from the start.

## The five rules the examiner kept applying

1. **One strand usually decides the band.** Best-fit balances strands, but the weakest strand drags the mark (05: strand 1 at 5–6, strand 2 at 3–4 → RD 4; 07: missing significance test alone cost the next band). Conversely, consistent mid-band strength across all strands earns the band's top mark (02's RD: every strand at 3–4 → 4).
2. **Small n is forgivable; no significance test is not.** Every sample that skipped testing was told so.
3. **Cheap wins:** correct graph scales, identified error bars, metric units, consistent decimal places, retrieval dates — none require more data, all move strands.
4. **Uncertainties earn marks only when interpreted** — in the conclusion, against the research question.
5. **Specificity is the currency of top bands** — in the RQ context, in the weaknesses, and in the improvements.
